Not sure about the equipment? Share what you know. We’ll help with the rest.

How quickly can I get a forklift and operator?

Send your date, ZIP code, and job details to get started. An agent will check equipment and operator availability and confirm the earliest available schedule with you.

Do I need to arrange equipment and an operator separately?

No. Choose Forklift + Operator and we coordinate both, including the equipment requirements and arrival details.

Can you unload a truck without a loading dock?

Tell us about the truck, load, and unloading area. We’ll review whether a forklift can safely handle the job and what equipment is needed.

What if I don’t know which forklift I need?

Share the load weight, lift height, surface, and available space. The booking form suggests equipment, and an agent confirms the final choice before booking.

Can I rent a forklift without an operator?

Yes. Choose Forklift Only when you already have a qualified operator. Choose Forklift + Operator when you need both.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on equipment, rental length, location, delivery, and operator needs. An agent confirms pricing before you commit to a booking.

Is my booking confirmed when I submit?

No. Your submission is a request. We’ll contact you to confirm equipment, availability, pricing, and scheduling.
