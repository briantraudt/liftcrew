Need the forklift, but have an operator?

Choose “Forklift Only” in the request form. Share the load, lift height, terrain, working space, date, and duration so we can review an appropriate equipment class and availability.

Forklift selection is about more than rated load capacity. The machine must also fit the travel path, surface, lift height, and load geometry. Our online suggestion is a starting point; the final equipment and quote are confirmed by LiftCrew.

Have these details ready

  • Heaviest item, including its pallet or crate
  • Load length and width, if known
  • Required lift height
  • Smooth floor, pavement, gravel, or mixed terrain
  • Narrowest gate, aisle, or doorway
  • Dates and number of days

Why the job details matter

Warehouse and indoor work

Floor condition, aisle width, and ventilation affect what equipment can work in the space.

Outdoor deliveries

Ground conditions, truck access, and load placement may point toward a different tire or capacity class.

One-off projects

Loading sold machinery, receiving a heavy delivery, or covering a broken site forklift can require a machine for a short window.
