Request checklist

Before you request a quote

You do not need every answer to get started. These are the details we’ll work through together.

  • What work needs to be done?
  • Where is the site and when is the work?
  • What are the heaviest and largest loads?
  • What lift height or reach is required?
  • Is the work indoors, outdoors, or both?
  • What are the site’s access and safety rules?

Frequently asked

Questions, answered.

Can I request just an operator or just equipment?

Yes. The booking form offers Forklift Only or Forklift + Operator. Tell us about your situation and we’ll review equipment and availability before confirming the request.

How far in advance should I reach out?

As early as possible gives more room to plan. Urgent requests are welcome, with availability dependent on your location, equipment, and schedule.

Can you support multi-day or ongoing work?

Yes, include the approximate duration and working hours in your request. We’ll discuss options based on the job.

What if I do not know the exact equipment I need?

Describe the materials, weights, lift height, ground conditions, and task. We can discuss the equipment requirements with you.

What does a quote depend on?

The equipment, operator requirements, site, location, dates, hours, and scope all affect the proposed plan and price.
