Application privacy

Effective October 6, 2026. This notice describes the information collected through LiftCrew’s operator portal.

What we collect

Account email, business and contact details, service areas, rates, equipment and operator records, insurance information, uploaded documents, application certifications, submission timestamps and review history. Authentication services also maintain security records. Only provide information you are authorized to share.

How it is used and shared

LiftCrew uses this information to evaluate providers, verify qualifications and coverage, administer the provider relationship and assignments, and maintain business and legal records. Authorized LiftCrew reviewers can access applications and documents. LiftCrew may verify records with the contacts you identify and share necessary qualification or insurance information with prospective clients and site representatives. Tax documents are not intended for client distribution. Hosting, authentication and storage providers process information to operate the portal. Information may also be disclosed when legally required.

Documents and sensitive information

Uploads are stored in a private application bucket with access controls. Do not place tax identification numbers or banking information in ordinary form fields. Submit a W-9 only in the designated tax-document upload. Do not upload bank details, Social Security cards, medical records or unrelated identity documents. For transport credentials, redact unnecessary birth dates, home addresses and identification numbers while preserving information needed to verify qualification.

Records and your choices

You can update your draft before submission and review your submitted information. Submitted application snapshots and certifications are retained for review and recordkeeping. Ask your LiftCrew business contact to correct, withdraw or delete application information. Requests are subject to applicable retention duties, disputes and legal requirements. Do not submit an application if you have no way to contact LiftCrew about it; return to the main site to establish a business contact first.

Electronic records

You need an email account and a current browser with JavaScript and PDF access. You can download the proposed agreement and print your application. Submitting electronically records your typed name, authority, certifications, agreement version and submission time. It does not execute an unfinished provider agreement or authorize dispatch.
